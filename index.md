## FAQ

**How do I get started?**
Hit Me Maybe teaches basic blackjack strategy in three steps:
1. **Flash Cards** — Study all 27 strategy rules at your own pace
2. **Quiz Mode** — Test your knowledge with multiple-choice questions
3. **Practice Mode** — Play real hands and get instant coaching on every decision

**What's included for free?**
The free version includes the full 27-card flash card deck in Study mode, a sample of Quiz mode (hard totals), limited Practice mode hands, and your overall accuracy stat.

**What does Premium unlock?**
The **Strategy Pro** upgrade — an auto-renewable subscription (monthly or yearly, with a free trial on the yearly plan) — unlocks full Quiz mode across all categories, unlimited Practice mode, the complete Stats dashboard with per-category accuracy and streaks, strategy drills, Career mode, and the monthly competition.

**How do I manage or cancel my subscription?**
Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID: open **Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions** on your device. You can also restore an active subscription in the app via **Settings → Restore Purchases** on any device signed in with the same Apple ID.

**How does practice mode work?**
Practice mode deals real blackjack hands from a simulated 6-deck shoe. After every decision (hit, stand, double, or split), the app evaluates your play against basic strategy. A green banner means you played optimally — a warning means there was a better move, and it tells you what it was.

**The app crashes or behaves unexpectedly?**
Please email us at the address above and include your iPhone/iPad model and iOS version so we can investigate.
