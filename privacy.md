# Privacy Policy for Hit Me Maybe: Blackjack Trainer

**Hit Me Maybe does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.**

- No analytics or tracking is used.
- No third-party SDKs are included.
- No accounts or sign-ups are required.
- All quiz results, practice stats, and game progress are stored locally on your device and are never sent to any server.

## In-App Purchases

Hit Me Maybe offers an optional premium upgrade ("Strategy Pro"), an auto-renewable subscription (monthly or yearly) processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. **Hit Me Maybe never receives, processes, or stores your payment information.** All purchase transactions are handled by Apple. Please refer to [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/privacy/) for details on how Apple handles purchase data.

## Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it at any time by uninstalling the app.
